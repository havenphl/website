get involved

There's more than one way to help.

A program this size runs on people showing up. Any of these matter.

most needed

volunteer

Sit on the floor and be a steady grown-up. We train you, and we ask for consistency over hours — the same face every week matters more than one big shift.

  • session assistants
  • read-aloud partners
  • supply prep & setup
ask about volunteering

partner with us

We're building HAVEN to be copied. If you run a shelter, a school, a clinic, or a family services program — or you teach art or movement — we'd like to talk.

  • shelters & transitional housing
  • schools & family services
  • art & movement practitioners
start a conversation

give supplies

In-kind gifts go straight into the room — crayons, paper, clay, books, snacks, yoga mats. We'll send you the current list before you buy anything.

  • art materials & paper
  • picture books (multilingual welcome)
  • snacks & floor cushions
request the supply list
