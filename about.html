Our Team

  1. Chloe, Connie, and Darlene met their freshman year of college.

  2. Though their wide-ranging individual interests in housing, youth education, health, and the arts have always been grounded in their commitment to service.

  3. Four years of working with children in Philadelphia led them to found HAVEN in response to the lack of consistent, structured enrichment for children living in family shelters.

  4. The memories of their own childhoods, of movement and storytelling, became ways to create joy.

Meet the Team

Chloe Chang

Director of Operations

Hailing from New Hyde Park, New York, Chloe combines her experience leading shelter- and school-based programs with her passion for creative expression and community-building — for HAVEN, she will lead the storytelling curriculum and support day-to-day fundraising, marketing, recruitment, and program operations.

Connie Ni

Director of Development

Originally from Chattanooga, Tennessee, Connie combines her passion for movement and youth empowerment with shelter-based health research — for HAVEN, she will lead dance curriculum, evaluate program outcomes, and turn what she learns on the ground into stronger support for kids and families.

Darlene Leohansson

Director of Programs

Born and raised in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania (#gobirds), Darlene uses her interests at the intersection of science and art to inform her work as an educator — she will lead the visual arts curriculum, build HAVEN’s model, and coordinate the program’s implementation to foster a safe space for children to be joyfully creative.
