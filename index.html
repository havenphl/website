Reimagining the shelter as a site for childhood, creativity, and growth.

In 2024, nearly a third of Philadelphians without housing were families with children. When a family lands in a shelter, the building is built for beds and paperwork — not for childhood.

These overburden shelters are ill-equipped to support the:

  1. 1critical development of children
  2. 2identity-building and autonomy of youth, and
  3. 3employment barriers of caregivers.

The Home for Arts, Voices, Enrichment, and Nurturing (HAVEN)
was built in response to this.

HAVEN is a non-profit after-school program integrating storytelling, dance therapy, and arts creation for children living in-shelter. We partner with Jane Addams Place, a family shelter in North Philadelphia, to bring enrichment directly to where families spend the most time.

Objective 1

Deliver a replicable, shelter-embedded academic and social-emotional enrichment curriculum for elementary-aged children.

Objective 2

Provide leadership opportunities for high-school youth living in shelters.

Objective 3

Reduce caregiver supervision burdens to support employment and housing stability.

By supporting a child, an adolescent, and their caregiver(s) — together — HAVEN strives to nurture the success of the whole family.
